# Living Knowledge Base

## Definition

A Living Knowledge Base is a customer-service knowledge system with a frozen, governed core and a programmable shell. It turns human-AI collaboration into three reusable signals: preference labels, quality labels, and result labels.

<div class="zh-trans">活知识库是具有“冻结、受治理核心”与“可编程外壳”的客服知识系统。它将人机协作转化为三类可复用信号：偏好标签、质量标签与结果标签。</div>

## How it works

Stable compliance and brand rules remain controlled, while retrieval, routing, and brand-specific configuration can evolve. Multi-brand expansion uses shared templates with override layers rather than separate workflow forks.

<div class="zh-trans">稳定的合规和品牌规则保持受控，检索、路由和品牌特定配置则可持续演进。多品牌扩展应采用共享模板加覆盖层，而非为每个客户分叉一套工作流。</div>

## Why it matters

The structure protects compliance and operational consistency while still allowing client delivery teams to learn from real conversations and improve brand performance over time.

<div class="zh-trans">这一结构既保障合规与运营一致性，又允许客户交付团队从真实对话中学习，并持续提升品牌运营效果。</div>
