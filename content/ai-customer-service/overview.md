# AI Customer Service and Shopping Guide

## Summary

This topic captures an AI-native private-domain shopping-guide platform for beauty and maternal-and-infant brands. Its advantage comes from combining real human-service conversations, validated operating SOPs, domain judgment, and an Agentic workflow—not from a generic chatbot alone.

<div class="zh-trans">本主题记录面向美妆及母婴品牌的 AI-native 私域导购平台。它的优势来自真实人工服务对话、经过验证的运营 SOP、领域判断与 Agentic 工作流的结合，而不仅是一套通用聊天机器人。</div>

## Key facts

- The current production baseline is a 33-node Dify workflow for maternal-and-infant shopping guidance.
- Abnormal health-symptom queries must be escalated to human agents; promotion and price logic must come from real-time systems.
- Current client context includes Aptamil, Mead Johnson, Estée Lauder, and Sulwhasoo.

<div class="zh-trans">关键事实：当前生产基线是一个包含 33 个节点的母婴智能导购 Dify 工作流；异常健康症状必须转人工，促销及价格逻辑必须来自实时系统；客户背景包括爱他美、美赞臣、雅诗兰黛和雪花秀。</div>
