# Claude Memory Export — AI Customer Service Materials

## Summary

The Claude memory export records customer-service and shopping-guide architecture, delivery learnings, workflow engineering rules, and client-facing material status. It covers Dify/ChatFlow, RAG, enterprise WeChat, and brand delivery in beauty and maternal-and-infant categories.

<div class="zh-trans">Claude 记忆导出记录了客服与导购的架构、交付经验、工作流工程规则和客户材料状态，覆盖 Dify/ChatFlow、RAG、企业微信及美妆与母婴品类的品牌交付。</div>

## Core points

- Medical or abnormal-health signals require mandatory human escalation.
- Promotions must use a real-time source of truth rather than a knowledge base.
- The known production gaps are missing similarity thresholds for some knowledge bases, missing HTTP fallbacks, and a planned output-side guardrail.

<div class="zh-trans">核心要点：医疗或异常健康信号必须强制转人工；促销信息必须使用实时的单一事实来源，而非知识库；已知生产缺口包括部分知识库尚未设相似度阈值、HTTP 节点缺少降级方案，以及待上线的输出侧护栏。</div>
