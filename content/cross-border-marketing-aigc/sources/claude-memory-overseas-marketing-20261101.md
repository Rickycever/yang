# Claude Memory Export — Overseas Marketing AIGC

## Summary

The export records AI-powered creative production and performance-marketing work for overseas marketing. It covers Astra, Meta creative deconstruction, supplier pricing, multilingual production, and a staged path from internal tooling to SaaS.

<div class="zh-trans">该导出记录了面向出海营销的 AI 创意生产与效果营销工作，涵盖 Astra、Meta 创意拆解、供应商定价、多语言生产，以及从内部工具逐步走向 SaaS 的路径。</div>

## Core points

- High-value AI applications are script generation and post-production adaptation; creative-angle selection, native-language audio review, and final visual QA remain human-led.
- Multilingual and Arabic RTL requirements are material operational differences in overseas delivery.
- A sustainable pricing model needs rate cards and WBS-based costing before customer quotations.

<div class="zh-trans">核心要点：AI 的高价值应用在脚本生成和后期适配；创意角度选择、母语音频审核和最终视觉质检仍须由人主导；多语言及阿拉伯语 RTL 要求是出海交付的重要运营差异；可持续的定价模型需在对客户报价前建立费率卡和基于 WBS 的成本核算。</div>
