# FDE Delivery Model

## Definition

FDE is Kuaizi's customer-success-led delivery model. It configures Atom, Skills, enterprise assets, and operating SOPs into a running video-commerce business system for a customer rather than selling isolated software functions.

<div class="zh-trans">FDE 是筷子以客户成功为导向的交付模型：不是销售孤立的软件功能，而是把 Atom、Skill、企业资产与运营 SOP 组装为客户可运行的视频商业业务系统。</div>

## How it works

Projects should begin with a clear SOP, accountable owner, measurable KPI, confirmed delivery resources, and confirmed availability of required MCP services. Successful cases are then distilled into reusable industry Skills and delivery components.

<div class="zh-trans">项目应从清晰 SOP、明确负责人、可衡量 KPI、已确认交付资源和已确认 MCP 服务可用时间开始。成功案例再沉淀为可复用的行业 Skill 与交付组件。</div>

## Why it matters

The commercial success metric is sustained customer consumption of KP credits through useful atomic services, video tokens, and high-value Skills. This ties delivery quality directly to scaled profitability rather than one-off project revenue.

<div class="zh-trans">其商业成功指标是客户持续消耗 KP 积分，包括原子服务、视频 Token 与高价值 Skill。这将交付质量直接连接到规模化盈利，而非一次性项目收入。</div>
