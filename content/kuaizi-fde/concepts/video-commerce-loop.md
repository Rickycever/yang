# Video-commerce Closed Loop

## Definition

The video-commerce closed loop is Kuaizi's shared value chain: brief → strategy → content production → distribution → performance data → iteration. It treats video as an operating asset whose business impact can be continuously improved.

<div class="zh-trans">视频商业闭环是筷子的共同价值链：brief → 策略 → 内容生产 → 分发 → 效果数据 → 迭代。它将视频视为可持续优化商业结果的运营资产。</div>

## How it works

Common implementation modules include asset centers, script and hit-video analysis, AI-assisted production, KOC or advertising distribution, approval controls, and performance feedback. Customer scenarios such as Sankeshu, Huanxin Recycle, short drama highlights, and GEO distribution each use different combinations of these modules.

<div class="zh-trans">常见落地模块包括素材资产中心、脚本与爆款解析、AI 辅助生产、KOC 或广告分发、审核控制和效果反馈。三棵树、焕新回收、短剧高光切片与 GEO 分发等客户场景，使用这些模块的不同组合。</div>

## Why it matters

The loop makes business outcomes, rather than content volume alone, the optimization target. It also creates reusable data and SOP assets that improve later delivery and can be turned into industry Skills.

<div class="zh-trans">该闭环把业务结果而非单纯内容数量作为优化目标，同时形成可复用的数据与 SOP 资产，用于提升后续交付，并可进一步沉淀为行业 Skill。</div>
