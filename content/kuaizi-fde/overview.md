# Kuaizi FDE and Video Commerce

## Summary

Kuaizi is evolving from video-production SaaS toward a video-commerce infrastructure model: Atom provides reusable tools, FDE assembles business systems, and KP consumption is the commercial flywheel. The operating goal is a measurable loop from brief to strategy, production, distribution, and data-led iteration.

<div class="zh-trans">筷子正从视频生产 SaaS 演进为视频商业基础设施模式：Atom 提供可复用工具，FDE 负责组装业务系统，KP 消耗构成商业飞轮。运营目标是建立从 brief、策略、生产、分发到数据驱动迭代的可衡量闭环。</div>

## Key facts

- Kuaizi Atom is positioned as an end-to-end video-commerce tool stack for AI Agents, with 8 business scenarios and 180+ tools as of late October 2026.
- Its delivery modes include SaaS, MCP connection to a customer-owned Agent, API, and private deployment.
- The FDE method is K.U.A.I.Z.I.: Kickoff, Unit Goals, Angle, Insight, Zero-to-Scale, and Ignite & Iterate.

<div class="zh-trans">关键事实：截至 2026 年 10 月底，Kuaizi Atom 的对外定位是面向 AI Agent 的全链路视频商业工具栈，覆盖 8 大业务场景、180+ 工具；支持 SaaS、通过 MCP 接入客户自有 Agent、API 与私有化部署。FDE 方法论为 K.U.A.I.Z.I.：Kickoff、Unit Goals、Angle、Insight、Zero-to-Scale、Ignite & Iterate。</div>
